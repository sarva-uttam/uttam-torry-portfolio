01 Opportunities

01 · 01 What opportunities are you looking for?

Graduate or junior software development roles where I can contribute reliably, learn from an experienced team and keep building production engineering skills. I am most interested in web applications, developer tools and product engineering.

01 · 02 Are you currently available for work?

Yes. I completed my Bachelor of Software Engineering in 2026 and am available now for graduate and junior roles.

01 · 03 Are you open to relocation or remote work?

Yes. I am based in Mauritius and open to relocation and international opportunities, as well as remote work.

02 Experience & skills

02 · 01 What is your professional experience level?

I am early in my professional engineering career. Rather than overstate experience, I focus on showing real project work that demonstrates how I think and build.

My earlier work in hospitality, tutoring and delivery built my adaptability, communication and reliability in demanding environments.

02 · 02 Which technologies do you work with?

HTML, CSS, JavaScript and TypeScript on the front end, with React and Next.js. On the back end I work with Node.js, PostgreSQL and Supabase. I also use Python and C++. For testing and workflow I use Git and GitHub, Vitest and Playwright.

02 · 03 What can you help a development team build?

Responsive, accessible user interfaces; TypeScript application code; APIs and PostgreSQL-backed features; automated tests with Vitest and Playwright; and clear technical documentation. I can also help with requirements analysis and keeping a codebase consistent.

03 Projects

03 · 01 Which project best represents your work?

Zordi Budget Tracker is the fullest example: an evidence-led tool built with Next.js, TypeScript and PostgreSQL that tracks items from initial promises through to verified outcomes.

ISeeCode (a visual, step-by-step code interpreter), Enveloped (personalised digital invitations) and Bwoo & Bloo’s Books (a structured educational-publishing system) each show a different side of how I work.

03 · 02 Why are some of your repositories private?

A few repositories are kept private while they are still in progress or contain work I do not have permission to publish. I am happy to demonstrate them and walk through the implementation on request.

03 · 03 Can you explain your technical decisions during an interview?

Yes. I can talk through the architecture, trade-offs and testing approach for any project in this portfolio, and walk through the code live if that is useful.

04 Working together

04 · 01 How do you approach a new development task?

I start by clarifying the requirements, then break the work into small, testable steps. I write tests alongside the implementation, keep changes reviewable, and document anything that is not obvious from the code. I would rather ask a question early than build the wrong thing.

04 · 02 Which languages do you speak?

English, French, Hindi and Russian.

04 · 03 How can someone contact you?

Email me at uttamtorry@gmail.com or reach out on LinkedIn. My code is on GitHub. There is also a “Start a conversation” option in the site footer.
